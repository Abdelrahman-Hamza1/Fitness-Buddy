## hijri-converter 2.3.2 — Umm al-Qura month starts

`lib/src/ramadan/umm_al_qura.dart` holds the first day of Ramadan and of
Shawwal for Hijri years 1440–1499, derived from the Umm al-Qura month-start
series (`MONTH_STARTS`, `src/hijri_converter/ummalqura.py`) of
[hijri-converter](https://pypi.org/project/hijri-converter/) 2.3.2.post1
(read with the epoch 1858-11-16; A41a D1). The series follows R.H. van Gent's
Umm al-Qura tables. The app shows this notice on About › Licences.
