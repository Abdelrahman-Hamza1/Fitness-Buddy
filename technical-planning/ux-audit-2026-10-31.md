## 1. Executive summary: the 10 most important problems

1. **The reported food-sheet shrink is confirmed.** Clearing the amount removes the macro line entirely. **UX-A01**
2. **Validation moves forms around.** Shared fields reserve no space for errors, affecting food creation, targets, measurements and sign-in. **UX-A02**
3. **Numbers can change surrounding geometry despite tabular digits.** Diary trailing values consume variable width; macro values can trigger an entirely different layout; workout PR labels squeeze inputs. **UX-A03–A05**
4. **Food-name preferences stop working in secondary flows.** Meal previews, edit sheets, Buddy, barcode pending items and feedback still contain English-only paths. **UX-A30–A40**
5. **Search can leave previous-query results tappable beneath newly typed text.** **UX-A41**
6. **Today lacks the specified pending-proposals strip.** Dismissing Buddy reduces discoverability of unfinished proposals. **UX-A62**
7. **Barcode recovery has gaps.** Returning from manual food creation has no camera-resume path, and manual-code entry lacks keyboard avoidance. **UX-A63–A64**
8. **Several workout screens show blank or misleading states during loading/errors.** Some failures become “no workouts,” “all rest,” or “session gone.” **UX-A42–A45**
9. **Some failures have no recovery UI.** Measurement entry can remain a skeleton; setup can remain an empty shell; adaptive cards disappear. **UX-A46–A48**
10. **Accessibility needs another focused pass.** Workout input rows lack responsive layouts, weekday targets are narrower than 48 dp, and some informative chart marks have insufficient contrast. **UX-A51–A59**

### Motion

Centralize standard easing `Cubic(.2, 0, 0, 1)` and emphasized deceleration `Cubic(.05, .7, .1, 1)` in `FbMotion`; avoid widget-specific curves. ## 4. What is already good

- **The visual foundation is coherent:** Embrace colors, tonal cards, typography, radii, button styles and sheet structure are centralized. Extend these recipes rather than creating screen-specific replacements.
- **Numeric groundwork exists:** `FbType.numeric` supplies tabular figures; Today’s calorie number already counts smoothly and its progress bars animate.

- **Food-language work is substantial:** search, recents, diary rows, saved-meal items and Your Foods already demonstrate the intended provider-based approach.
- **Wellbeing presentation is strong:** over-target styling is neutral, estimates are identified, and the reviewed English catalogs contain none of the prohibited moralizing food terms.
- **Many touch targets and semantics are deliberate:** custom keypad keys, slide dots, swipe semantics and reorder feedback are good models.
- **Several flows preserve intent well:** stale-data handling, Undo feedback, pinned sheet actions and sign-in cancellation already provide patterns to copy elsewhere.
