## S02 — Dart client generator (2026-10-12)

Proven hands-on in a scratch pub workspace (Dart 3.13.5 / Flutter 3.47.7, with a Flutter member to reproduce the SDK's pins) against an OpenAPI 3.1 fixture: `GET /v1/config` (maps of bool/number, `type: [string, 'null']`), RFC 9457 `ProblemDetails`, and an `Intent` `oneOf` + `discriminator.mapping` with 3 variants (`kind` as `const`). **Recommendation: `swagger_parser` 1.45.0 in freezed mode** (retrofit on dio), plus one pre-generation transform that strips `format: date` from a temporary copy of the bundled contract. **Runner-up: `tonik` 0.10.0.** Not suitable: `openapi-generator` dart-dio.
