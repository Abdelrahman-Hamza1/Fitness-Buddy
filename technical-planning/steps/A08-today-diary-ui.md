## Scope
- **Today layout** (`spec/16` §1): date header with ‹ ›, the big number area (eaten kcal until targets exist), macro line, slot sections with items, kcal and a compact macro line per slot, day totals.
- **Bottom area:** the quick-add chip row placeholder (filled in A09), the **input bar shell** with camera and barcode buttons (behaviour in A22/A28). Tapping the barcode/camera buttons shows "coming soon" in dev builds only.
- **Item expand** (DY-021): amount, full nutrients, source badge, assumptions, and direct edits of amount / food (via search) / slot / time, and **override a nutrient value** (recorded as "edited by you", AI-072). All direct actions with undo.
- **Swipe** (DY-022): delete (undo) or move to another slot.
- **Dish groups** (DY-024): collapsible under the dish name.
- **Meal actions** (DY-023): copy a meal to another day/slot, save as a saved meal (list UI in A09).
- **Day navigation** (HM-009): swipe left/right, date picker. Past days use the same layout.
- **States:** empty day ("Tell Buddy what you had for breakfast" + search), skeletons only where needed, offline/pending banners (calm, UX-053).
- Add-food entry points: "+" per slot → search (A07) with that slot preselected.

## Out of scope

### 1. Summary
After A08, Today is the real home screen. A fixed date header (‹ › and a tappable date that opens a date picker) sits above one page per day, swiped left/right (HM-009). Each page shows the day's eaten kcal and macro totals on top (targets come in A13), then the meal slots as compact rows (name, item summary, kcal, "+"). A tap expands a slot to its items and its macro line. Dish groups show under the dish name, expanded inside an opened slot and collapsible (DY-024). Tapping an item expands its details: amount, full nutrients, badge, assumptions, time. In A08b these details become editable (amount, food via search, slot, time, and an "edited by you" nutrient override, AI-072). Swiping an item deletes it or moves it to another slot (DY-022). Each slot's menu copies the meal to another day or slot, or saves it as a saved meal (DY-023). Every change is a direct action with a 5 s undo (UX-030). Neighbouring days are prefetched, so a swipe shows data in its first frame. The bottom area holds the input bar: it opens food search until Buddy arrives in A22, and in dev builds it also has camera/barcode buttons that say "coming soon".

| D12 | **Concurrency and staleness rules.** (1) Every command takes the `DiaryEntry` / `VersionedId` from the layout the user acted on (the last rendered emission). The stream re-emits after every write, so that version is current except during a real race. (2) **One in-flight command per entry**: `DiaryActionsController.pending` (ids). That row's controls are disabled while pending, and a second tap is dropped, not queued. (3) `Rejected(VersionConflict \| RowNotFound \| InvalidOp(sourceChanged))` → plain snackbar "That item changed. Nothing was saved." (or "…copied/saved" for meals). No automatic retry with a fresh version: the user sees the new data first. (4) **Open sheets** (amount, override, copy, save, change food, move) keep the version they opened with. They watch the entry through the **authoritative by-ID read `DiaryRepository.watchEntry(id)`** (A08b; for the move sheet in A08a, the command result is enough): the live row wherever it now is (any date or slot), or `null` when tombstoned (PA4). `null` → the sheet shows "This item was removed" and Save is disabled. A **move** to another slot or day keeps the sheet usable: same entry, new version. A different version → Save would conflict, so the sheet shows "This item changed" and a "Reload" action that rebases on the current entry **and keeps the typed value** (UX-052). Copy/save sheets check their source the same way, through the section of their own date (`dayViewProvider(date)` is held by the open sheet) plus PA2's in-transaction check. (5) **Undo** goes through `UndoStack.undo`: `conflict` → "Can't undo: it changed since." `expired` → "Too late to undo." `unknown` → nothing. (6) **Day streams are latest-wins**: `DiaryRepository._watch` gets A07's history pattern (one read in flight, one dirty flag, newest result only), so a write burst never queues a read per write. (7) The "+" route carries the **viewed date**, so an item added at 00:01 from a page opened at 23:59 lands on the page's day (A07 `EntryWhen.onDay`). **State ownership (PA4):** (8) `DiaryActionsController` and `DiaryViewState` are **keepAlive** (app lifetime), so pending commands and marks outlive the day providers, and swiping beyond the 3-day window can't drop them. `pending` is cleared in the command's `finally`, whatever happens to the providers. (9) **Hidden marks** `(entryId, version, originDate)` are matched by **id + version**, so another day's emission lacking the id proves nothing and clears nothing, a filtered layout is never used as evidence, and an Undo (whose restore bumps the version, A06) shows the revived row at once anywhere. A mark is removed when (a) the command is **rejected** (now), or (b) the **raw** `watchDay(originDate)` emission (before the hidden filter; `dayViewProvider` exposes it to the controller) has no live row with that id at that version, or (c) it is older than the undo window + 10 s, because a committed delete leaves a tombstone that no read returns. (10) **Expansion** stores `(date, entryId)`. It is cleared only when the raw emission of **that date** has no such entry. If the expanded entry is **moved to another slot**, it stays expanded and its new slot opens; if it moves to another day or is deleted, its key clears on that date's next raw emission | A07's plan review found races and stale-result gaps. These rules make every async path explicit and testable. A deletion is proven only by the row's own day or by version, never by a neighbouring day or a filtered list (PA4) |
