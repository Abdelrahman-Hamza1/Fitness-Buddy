**Goal.** The fastest paths for repeat eating: your own foods, named saved meals, and quick-add chips that log a repeat meal in at most two taps, with no AI involved.

**Spec:** FD-070 (form path), FD-071, FD-073, FD-012 (user warning), DY-030, DY-031 (tap path), UX-004, MN-021 · **Decisions:** TD-011

## Scope
- **Personal foods:** a form to create, edit, rename, **merge duplicates** and delete (FD-073: history keeps its logged values). Names, aliases, per-100 g or per-serving entry (converted), servings with grams, brand, barcode(s) field (used by A28). A gentle sanity warning (FD-012).
- **Saved meals:** list, detail, rename, delete. **Log by tap** = a direct action with undo into the chosen slot (DY-031). Created from any logged meal (A08 action).
- **Quick-add chips** (DY-030): a ranking model over history (time of day, current slot, weekday, frequency, recency) producing the top chips (foods, saved meals, and later open batches). One tap logs with the last-used amount + undo. A "+" chip opens search.
- Personal foods appear first in search (A07 ranking) with the ⓨ badge.

## Out of scope
- Creating personal foods by chat or label (A27/A28). Batches (A27).

## Fixed by the plan
- Repeat logging never calls the AI (MN-021). Chips are direct actions, not proposals (D-079).

## Decide in the low-level plan
- The chip ranking formula (documented, unit-tested with scenarios).
- The merge semantics (which food survives, how aliases combine).

## Exit criteria
- [ ] A repeat meal logged in ≤ 2 taps from Today (integration test counts taps) (UX-004).
- [ ] Ranking tests: breakfast chips in the morning, dinner chips in the evening, a frequent item outranks a one-off.
- [x] Merge test: diary entries referencing the merged food keep their snapshots. (A09a)
- [ ] Goldens for the personal food form and the chip row. (form: A09a ✅; chip row: A09b)

### 1. Summary
After A09 the fastest paths for repeat eating exist, all offline and with no AI (MN-021). **Your foods** (Me › Your foods, and "Create your own food" from a search with no match) is a form to create, edit, rename, delete and merge personal foods: names and aliases, values per 100 g/ml or per serving (converted), servings, brand, barcodes (stored for A28), state, with a gentle FD-012 warning. Personal foods rank first in search with the ⓨ badge, and editing, merging or deleting one never changes what's already logged (FD-073). **Saved meals** (Me › Saved meals) get a list and a detail screen with rename, delete and **Log** into a chosen meal and day, a direct action with undo (DY-031). **Quick-add chips** above Today's input bar show the foods and saved meals you most likely eat now, ranked from your last 60 days (time of day, slot, weekday, frequency, recency). One tap logs the last-used amount with undo, so a repeat meal takes 1 tap (UX-004). A "+" chip opens search. Every repeat log carries the versions of what the user saw and writes nothing if it changed meanwhile (PA1).

**3.0 Shared `personalFood` rules (F1).** One table, cited by the app (`PersonalFoodBounds` + `PersonalFoodDraft.validate`, WP2) and by B17's server validator; a test on each side reads the same numbers (the app's from `PersonalFoodBounds`, B17's from its validator constants), and the contract carries the subset its exporter can express (D3).
