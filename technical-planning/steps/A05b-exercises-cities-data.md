**Goal.** Fill the empty `exercises` and `cities` tables of catalog pack schema v1 with reviewed data: the exercise library seed (WO-001, WO-002) and the cities subset (LC-033), validated and released by A05's pipeline.

**Spec:** WO-001, WO-002 (exercise data), LC-033 (city data) · **Decisions:** TD-010, TD-071, TD-072

## Scope
- `data/exercises.yaml` (≈ 100 exercises with types, equipment variants as separate entries, EN names and aliases), per A05 D15.
- `data/cities.tsv` (a GeoNames subset: Palestine, Jordan, region + major world cities, with coordinates and time zones), generated by `fb_catalog import-cities` with `data/geonames/sources.yaml`, per A05 D16.
- Loaders, validation and pack/load-file rows for both in `tools/catalog` (the tables and `exercises.jsonl` / `cities.jsonl` already exist, empty, from A05). Pack schema v1 keeps the empty tables, so no schema change.

## Out of scope
- The pipeline, CI and app code (A05). The workout domain and UI (A36, A37). City search UI and prayer times (A40, A41).

## Fixed by the plan
- IDs are `ex:<slug>` and `city:<geonameid>`, stable forever (A05 D1–D2, TD-071). English names only (WO-001). GeoNames is CC BY 4.0 with attribution (A05 D18).

## Exit criteria
- [ ] `data/exercises.yaml` (≈ 100 exercises, equipment variants separate, EN aliases) validates and is searchable in the pack (WO-001, WO-002).
- [ ] `data/cities.tsv` has coordinates and a valid IANA zone per row, and the owner's city is present (LC-033). Counts in the Outcome.
- [ ] `make catalog` and `make catalog-check` pass. The pack stays under the 10 MB budget.
