## Low-level plan
_Written in Phase B (2026-10-23) by the step-planner, against `main` at ba25697 (A01–A08b, A12, A20 merged) and A09's approved plan on `step/A09a-personal-foods` (cf8eabe; A09a in implementation). **Split proposed (orchestrator decides under TD-037, §8): A10a "Settings, units & About" = WP1–WP4; A10b "Meal slot editor" = WP5–WP7, after A10a merges.** No contract change, no migration (D2). **Revised after the plan review (Codex read-only, 2026-10-23: rework; PA1–PA6, all accepted by the orchestrator; list at the end). Orchestrator decision: A11 depends on A09b + A10a; A13 depends on A10a and A10b (§8 lists the board rows).**_

_**Tiers.** **A10a: Normal.** Every write goes through the existing, tested `SettingsOp` family and `SettingsOpHandler` (A01 D16) via `SettingsStore.put/reset` and undo through `ChangeSetApplier.revert`. No op, handler, applier, table, migration or contract change; the only `core/settings` change is a codec for an optional string (encode/decode, no write-path logic). Everything else is read path (catalog cities, pack credits), pure display rules and UI. **A10b: Risky** (user-data write path, `PROCESS.md` A.2): a new op family `mealSlot` and its handler, including `RemoveMealSlot`, which rewrites the slot of every live entry and dish group in that slot in one change set under exact source preconditions (PA1), plus a `SlotAssigner` rule change (D9) and the slot handling of the A07/A08b sheets that capture slot choices (PA3). So A10b gets a separate `security-reviewer` pass, `technical-planning/steps/evidence/A10b-verification.md`, and Opus for any Claude-implemented WP that touches the write path (this plan gives all of it to Codex). If A10a turns out to need an op or handler change, that piece moves to A10b (the A08a/A08b rule)._

### 1. Summary
After A10 the Me tab has its real structure: sections in UX-010 order, each shown once its step exists (A09's "Food" section, then **Settings** and **About** now; Profile/Targets, Buddy remembers and Account rows arrive with A13, A26 and A33). Settings are rows on Me, so each is 2 taps from Today: **Units** (metric/imperial; every displayed food weight and volume follows it, stored values never change), **Week starts on** (default Saturday, feeding `WeekCalendar`), **Halal** (on), **Theme** (system/light/dark, backlog #75), **City** (a search-first picker over the pack's 2,276 GeoNames cities, EN or AR). **About** shows the version, the food-data version, the active data credits (USDA, GeoNames), open-source licences and SF-040's "not medical advice" line. All settings are synced `settings_kv` rows written through the applier, each with a 5 s undo. In A10b, **Meal times** lets the user rename, add, remove (moving that meal's items to another meal), reorder and set time windows; new entries are auto-assigned with the new windows, and a narrower window inside a wider one wins (D9).
